GARAGEMETRIC CLOUD

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your GarageMetric Cloud account and associated cloud data either from the app or directly from this page.

Delete from the GarageMetric app

  1. Open GarageMetric and sign in.
  2. Open More → Cloud Account.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm the second destructive prompt.

After successful deletion, the app signs out and clears the deleted account workspace on the current device.

Delete from the web

This form authenticates directly with GarageMetric's Supabase project. Your password is sent to Supabase Auth over HTTPS and is not stored by this GitHub Pages website.

Account deletion is irreversible. Use this form only for an account you intend to permanently delete.

What is deleted

  • Your GarageMetric Cloud authentication account.
  • Synced vehicle, maintenance, service-history and reminder records associated with the account.
  • GarageMetric cloud backup data associated with the account.
  • Private cloud attachments stored for that account.

If you delete through the app, the current device's account workspace and scheduled GarageMetric reminders are also cleared. Other devices can retain local offline copies until their GarageMetric application data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; after cloud account deletion, those devices can no longer authenticate to the deleted account.

Retention after deletion

GarageMetric removes the active account and associated application data when deletion succeeds. Infrastructure providers may retain limited backup, security or audit information temporarily where necessary for service reliability, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Need help?

For account-deletion questions, contact the GarageMetric developer through github.com/uglov. Never post your password or authentication tokens publicly.
