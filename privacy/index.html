Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Applies to GarageMetric 1.0 and this website.

GarageMetric is a vehicle maintenance and service-history application. This policy explains what information the app processes, why it is used, where cloud data is stored, and how you can delete it.

1. Information GarageMetric processes

Account information

If you create a GarageMetric Cloud account, the authentication service processes your email address, an account identifier, authentication session information and security metadata required to sign you in. GarageMetric does not receive your plaintext password.

Garage and maintenance information

Information you enter can include vehicle details such as make, model, year, nickname, VIN, plate, odometer, operating hours or charge cycles; maintenance plans; service history; parts and labor costs; notes; and reminder settings.

Photos and documents

When you choose to attach a photo or document to a service record, GarageMetric processes the selected file. Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you use the related attachment feature. Files are stored locally and may also be uploaded to your private cloud account when Cloud Sync or Cloud Backup is used.

Synchronization metadata

GarageMetric uses technical metadata such as generated device IDs, entity versions, synchronization revisions and timestamps to reconcile changes safely between signed-in devices.

2. How the information is used

  • To provide garage, maintenance, service-history and reminder features.
  • To authenticate your GarageMetric Cloud account.
  • To synchronize your data across your signed-in devices.
  • To create and restore private cloud backups when you request them.
  • To store and synchronize service attachments you choose to upload.
  • To detect and resolve synchronization conflicts without silently overwriting changes.

3. Local data and cloud services

GarageMetric is designed to remain useful offline. Local application data is stored on your device. If you sign in and use cloud features, GarageMetric uses Supabase for authentication, database services and private file storage.

Cloud data may be processed on infrastructure operated by Supabase and its subprocessors. Network traffic is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Cloud records are account-scoped and service attachments are stored in private storage rather than a public file bucket.

4. Quick Add and AI

In GarageMetric 1.0, Quick Add uses the local parser when the production remote-AI endpoint is not enabled. Version 1.0 does not expose remote voice-AI processing in the production app. If a future GarageMetric release enables remote AI processing, this policy will be updated before that processing is introduced.

5. Notifications

GarageMetric can schedule maintenance and custom reminders on your device. Notification permission is used for those reminders. GarageMetric 1.0 does not use notification permission for advertising.

6. Advertising, sale of data and tracking

GarageMetric 1.0 does not contain advertising SDKs, does not sell your personal information, and does not use your garage data for cross-app advertising or behavioral advertising.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local information remains on a device until you delete it, clear the application data, delete the related account workspace, or uninstall the app. Cloud information remains associated with your account until it is deleted through GarageMetric features or the account is deleted.

When account deletion succeeds, GarageMetric deletes the cloud authentication account, synchronized GarageMetric records, cloud backups and private cloud attachments associated with that account. The current device's account workspace is also cleared. Infrastructure providers may retain limited backup or security records temporarily where required for service reliability, abuse prevention or legal compliance.

See the Account Deletion page for the in-app and web deletion options.

8. Security

GarageMetric uses authenticated access, account-scoped database rules, private object storage and encrypted network transport for cloud features. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

GarageMetric is intended for vehicle owners and maintenance tracking and is not designed for children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when GarageMetric adds or changes features, service providers or data-processing practices. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the GarageMetric developer through the public developer profile at github.com/uglov. Do not post passwords, authentication tokens or private garage records in a public GitHub issue.
